What remains unsolved (from the papers and vendor statements above): single-layer separation from a stack without a task-specific gripper; collar/neckband alignment to a few millimetres by a learned policy; recovery from severe failures without a human deciding to abort; reliable transfer across embodiments and tasks without catastrophic forgetting; RGB sim-to-real for deformables (SimWeaver claims zero-shot RGB VLA policies from 200 simulated demos, unverified beyond the paper [142]); and any published learned policy that sews.

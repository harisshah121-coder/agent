### F6. Garment turning (inside-out)

No automatic T-shirt turning machine with published specifications was found in three attempts. Ngai Shing lists only trouser and pocket vacuum reversing machines [S54]; SMIT (Bangladesh) lists a jeans turning machine [S59]. Status: unknown. Design consequence: sequence the ST05/ST06 operations so the garment is right-side-out at the end (hems and neckband can be sewn with the garment right-side-out on cylinder-bed machines), or turn by vacuum tube with the ST04 gripper as a custom skill; both must be validated (see Open questions).

Cycle-time reality (est., basis: machine speeds above): a 5,000-stitch left-chest logo at a sustained 800–1,000 spm takes 5–6 min plus trims and colour changes, i.e. ≈ 8–10 pieces/h on one head. For 100 shirts per 12 h shift, one single head keeps pace only if every shirt carries ≤ ~6,000 stitches; a 2- or 4-head TMEZ-KC is needed for denser work, as the white paper already warns.

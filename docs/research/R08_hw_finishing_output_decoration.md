### F6. Garment turning (inside-out)

No automatic T-shirt turning machine with published specifications was found in three attempts. Ngai Shing lists only trouser and pocket vacuum reversing machines [S54]; SMIT (Bangladesh) lists a jeans turning machine [S59]. Status: unknown. Design consequence: sequence the ST05/ST06 operations so the garment is right-side-out at the end (hems and neckband can be sewn with the garment right-side-out on cylinder-bed machines), or turn by vacuum tube with the ST04 gripper as a custom skill; both must be validated (see Open questions).

Is it needed? For a cotton/elastane jersey T-shirt no construction step requires pressing (no fusing, no creases). Steam finishing is cosmetic (removes handling wrinkles before folding) and all automatic folders accept an unpressed, flat-laid garment. The white paper already classes "retail pressing, folding and packaging" as optional modules (white paper lines 367 and 1435). Recommendation in §Recommendation: no press in the baseline; a VEIT UF 30 is the cheapest add-on if a premium "steamed" presentation is sold, at the cost of a steam supply (≈ 15 kg/h) and a manual or robotic dressing step that is itself unsolved.

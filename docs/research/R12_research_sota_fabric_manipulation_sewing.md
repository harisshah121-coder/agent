### 8. Solved vs unsolved (as of October 2026)

Solved or demonstrated with published numbers:
- Unfolding/smoothing of crumpled garments with bimanual fling + quasi-static primitives: 80–95 % coverage in a few actions (FlingBot, DextAIRity, ClothFunnels, UniFolding).
- Folding of T-shirts at research-lab reliability: 60–93 % per-episode (UniFolding 60 % on unseen T-shirts, SpeedFolding 93 % on a trained T-shirt, FoldNet++ >90 % on unseen T-shirts with synthetic-only training).
- 24-hour unattended folding of napkins at 99.4 % with recovery (DYNA-1), and commercial laundromat deployments (Weave + π0.5/0.6) with interventions still counted per load.
- Millimetre-level alignment of two flat plies to CAD sewing lines (Dong 2026) and zero-shot texture-matched placement (texture-matching RA-L 2025).
- Straight-seam robotic sewing with differential stretch (Jin 2025) and fixture-free dual-arm feeding of prepared pairs (Tang 2025); closed-loop visual-servoed sewing of any 2D seam on stiffened fabric (Siemens/Sewbo), extended in 2026 to curvilinear tangential sewing.
- Single-ply separation of permeable technical textiles at >99 % with sensed low-pressure suction; >96.7 % destacking with a rotational-pinch soft gripper on six garment fabrics.
- Drape-test-based estimation of bending/stretch parameters for graphics-grade simulators (Feng 2022, Ju 2024).

Unsolved or without production evidence:
- Any published, audited end-to-end robotic T-shirt assembly throughput, defect rate or 12-hour unattended run (SoftWear's and Silana's figures are vendor claims; the ARM T-shirt project published no results page).
- Overlock/coverstitch (504/406) sewing of light stretch knits by robots: all academic sewing papers above use lockstitch-class machines or do not state stitch class; tube loading (sleeve/body side seam) by robot is undemonstrated in the literature found.
- Knit-specific single-ply pick-up off a vacuum cutting bed with verified "exactly one ply, no stretch" at >99.9 %; no paper reports this.
- Per-garment intervention probabilities near the 0.01 % the white paper derives for 95 %-confidence 500-garment batches; best public figure is 0.6 % per item (DYNA-1).
- Sim-to-real for knit dynamics: RGBench shows mainstream simulators have 20 % higher error than a purpose-built one; Isaac FEM cloth availability was still pending in the Isaac Lab discussion found.
- Real-time (sewing-speed) garment state estimation with foundation-model keypoints (CLASP states real-time perception "still challenging").

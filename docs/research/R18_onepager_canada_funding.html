The catch

The R&D refunds only go to Canadian-controlled companies. If Saudi investors control us, or we become a subsidiary of a US parent, we lose roughly CAD 0.5–1M a year.

The setup that keeps it

  • Canadian parent company, founder-controlled through the seed.
  • Saudi and US money as non-controlling stakes.
  • US subsidiary for US sales and US-facing investors.
  • If we flip to a US parent later, file the R&D claims first.

Next steps

  1. Incorporate as an Ontario Canadian-controlled company; tax lawyer reviews the cap table before the first cheque.
  2. Book an NRC IRAP advisor call; start R&D timesheets from day one.
  3. Sign a university partner (fabric handling or vision) for Mitacs and NSERC.
  4. Line up a uniform or apparel manufacturer as partner for the next NGen or Scale AI call.

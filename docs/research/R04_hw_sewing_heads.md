### F1. What the T-shirt needs from ST05/ST06 (stitch classes and seam roles)

- Structural knit seams (shoulder, side/sleeve-underarm, sleeve set, neckband attach): overlock 514 (2-needle 4-thread) or 504 (1-needle 3-thread); Coats specifies 14–16 stitches per 3 cm for knitted T-shirt overlock, needle Nm 70–90 [99][100].
- Hems (sleeve, bottom) and neck topstitch/cover: coverstitch 406 (2-needle) or 602 (3-needle with top cover), 14–18 stitches per 3 cm [99][100]; a left-hand fabric trimmer subclass (JUKI H22/H23, Yamato VG3721 "left knife hemming", Jack W4 /08) trims the hem allowance in line with the seam [81][22][28].
- Lockstitch 301 is needed only for labels, decorative topstitch and future woven garments; Coats lists 12–14 stitches per 3 cm with Epic Tex 24/30 [99]. For the premium T-shirt it is an optional head.
- Kansai Special's published T-shirt operation map confirms the industry mapping: neck rib attaching on a 2-needle 4-thread top-feed cylinder-bed overlock (JJT5014GH-01M), shoulder tape on a feed-off-the-arm double chainstitch (SQ7702FB-L-TC2), and bottom hemming on a cylinder-bed bottom-cover automat (NR9803GALK-ABH-4) [89].
